Detroit Dumpster Placement & Permit Requirements

In the City of Detroit, roll-off containers placed on your private driveway or property do not require a permit. However, if you need to place a dumpster on a public street, berm, or sidewalk, a DPW Right-of-Way permit is required under Detroit City Code Section 43, Division 2. For construction projects in the Central Business District (between I-75, I-375, Lodge Freeway, and the Detroit River), contact Traffic Engineering first.
